# Four Spare Quills

*A short Malfoid scene draft: a first-year packs for Hogwarts with one extra quill for everyone she has not met yet.*

**INT. MALFOY HOME — BEDROOM — DAY**

An open trunk sits on the floor. Malfoy packs. Her mother watches from the doorway.

**MALFOY:** Four spare quills.

**MOTHER:** Four?

**MALFOY:** One for me. Three for people who forget.

**MOTHER:** Do you know someone going?

**MALFOY:** Not yet. I'm being prepared.

The trunk is full of clothes and books. Malfoy lays a long four-slot quill case across the top and tries the lid. The rigid case holds it open by a hand's width. It slips; quills roll across the rug. One stops under the bed.

**MALFOY:** The trunk is badly made.

**MOTHER:** Would you like me to hold it?

**MALFOY:** No. I can—

She pushes the case back into the trunk. The lid lifts again.

**MALFOY:** Mother. Hold this side, please.

Her mother holds the lid while Malfoy pulls the case out. She retrieves the quill under the bed, puts one in her pocket, and leaves the other three in their case on the desk. The trunk closes neatly.

**MOTHER:** Only one?

**MALFOY:** I can lend one. I'm not opening a shop.
