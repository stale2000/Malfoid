# One Race

*A Malfoid scene draft: Draconia tries to restart a contest before she is ready to ask Harry why he is still angry.*

**EXT. HOGWARTS — COURTYARD — DAY**

Several school days later. Harry crosses with a book. Malfoy waits near an archway. She sees him.

**MALFOY:** I can beat you to that arch.

**HARRY:** You can try.

She runs before he has finished the sentence.

She reaches the arch and turns, pleased.

Harry is still across the courtyard.

**HARRY:** You won.

**MALFOY:** You didn't start.

**HARRY:** You didn't wait.

Her smile fades. She walks back to him.

**MALFOY:** Count this time?

**HARRY:** I'm still angry about Neville.

**MALFOY:** I know.

Harry sets his book on a bench.

**HARRY:** One race. On three.

She takes her place beside him.

**HARRY:** One. Two. Three.

She waits for three. They run.

Harry reaches the arch first.

Malfoy comes up beside him.

**MALFOY:** Again?

Harry returns for his book.

**HARRY:** Maybe after class.

Malfoy nods once and heads toward class. Harry picks up his book.
