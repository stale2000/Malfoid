# Artwork and video notes

The illustrations are AI-generated directly from text prompts for this unofficial fan project. No movie frames, fan art, or voice samples were used to generate them.

- `images/mirror-erised-anime-key-art.png` — anime-style key art for the Draconia/Harry Mirror scene, generated for the README.
- `images/four-spare-quills-anime-art.png` — story illustration for the scene draft at `../stories/four-spare-quills.md`.
- `images/mirror-erised-v5-contact-sheet.png` — contact sheet from the Mirror of Erised anime scene V5.
- `video/mirror-erised-v5-silent-preview.mp4` — the V5 visual track with its dialogue and audio removed. 15 seconds, 1376×768 at 24 fps.

The scene animation and contact sheet were generated with MiniMax H3 under ComfyUI's H3 license. The public video contains no cloned voice audio. The H3 model weights, source movie, and source soundtrack are not included.

The scene images and video are fan works using Harry Potter characters and settings. The public preview contains no source movie footage, soundtrack, or cloned voices. See [LICENSES.md](../LICENSES.md) for reuse terms.
