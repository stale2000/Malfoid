# First-year crush-scene ideas

**Status:** Ideation, October 5, 2026. This is a small, source-linked sample of public posts and fan discussion, not a survey of the fandom or a decision to change the feature.

## The strongest new-scene seed: the Mirror joke

A viral fanfic-style X post turns the Mirror of Erised into a crush reveal: Draconia sees herself beside Harry, then hides the vulnerability with a joke that the Mirror must be broken. A longer related post builds a comic denial spiral around her assuming her thoughts are caused by a spell. An animated proof of concept presents that frustration in broad anime comedy.

- [Kyle / @Brownkyl1’s Mirror post](https://x.com/Brownkyl1/status/2106529144477462616) — text and a short embedded scene.
- [moonlightbro’s longer setup](https://x.com/moonlightbro/status/2106494925265084611) — the mistaken-curse premise and escalating search.
- [Kevin Le Guillou’s animation test](https://x.com/kevinleguillou/status/2106864655989612792) — a visual treatment of the library frustration.

These are individual creators’ fanworks. They are useful as signals for a comic mechanism—notice a crush, invent an explanation, over-investigate, get exposed, cover with a taunt—not as material to reproduce. Avoid lifting dialogue, shots, fan art, or audio.

## Existing first-year beats that can carry the same energy

- **Train:** she wants Harry to choose her company; insulting Ron makes Harry decline. Her attempt to regain composure can carry the brief joke. Preserve Harry’s choice of Ron.
- **Midnight duel:** her confidence that she can control the encounter meets the real risk of being caught. Keep the deception consequential; do not recast it as an affectionate trick.
- **Fair-start race:** competitive fixation can grow into a mutually chosen game only after the story earns it. It does not erase the harm to Neville.
- **Robes shop:** an optional book-continuity seed, not a scene in the supplied first film. Any film adaptation would need to establish it as new material.

The current preference is to tune existing first-year scenes where possible and treat the Mirror sequence as an optional new scene requiring its own continuity decision.

## Questions before advancing the Mirror idea

1. Where can a later Mirror visit occur without interrupting Harry seeing his parents or replacing Ron’s vision?
2. Does the film’s Mirror relocation leave a plausible later visit, or does this require a deliberate adaptation change?
3. Can Draconia’s crush reveal be funny while Harry remains focused on the Mirror’s revelation and his family grief?
4. What does she choose after the taunt, so the scene changes her behavior instead of functioning as a detachable gag?
