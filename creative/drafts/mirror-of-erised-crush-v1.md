# Mirror of Erised crush scene — working draft v1

**Status:** Working draft / placement unresolved. This is an optional scene experiment, not locked feature screenplay coverage.

## Dramatic idea

Draconia follows an unusually intense curiosity about Harry to the Mirror of Erised. She would rather blame a broken mirror than admit that her desire is to be beside him. Harry explains what the Mirror does with sincere awkwardness; her joke briefly exposes her feelings, then gives her a way to recover her pride.

## Continuity guardrails

- This is a later visit. Harry’s first vision of his parents and his grief must play without a crush joke over it.
- Do not remove or replace Ron’s vision, Dumbledore’s explanation, or the consequences of Harry’s attachment to the Mirror.
- The film’s Mirror relocation makes this placement a decision still to be solved. Consider a second visit only if it fits the revised timeline; otherwise park the idea.
- Both characters are first-years. Keep the humor and affection age-appropriate, and do not imply Harry has harmed or magically controlled her.

## Scene attempt

**INT. HOGWARTS CLASSROOM — NIGHT**

The room is empty except for the tall gold-framed Mirror. Harry and Draconia stand several feet apart, watching their reflections. In the glass, the two of them appear side by side, quietly happy.

Harry notices her looking from the reflection to him. He blushes and gestures toward the frame.

**HARRY:** It’s supposed to show you your heart’s greatest desire. It’s called the Mirror of Erised. Dumbledore told me about it.

Draconia studies the reflection for one beat. She lifts her chin, as if she has caught the Mirror making a mistake.

**DRACONIA:** Well then your mirror must be broken, Potter, because I only see you and me.

A small giggle escapes her. Harry looks at the reflection, surprised, then back at her. Draconia tries not to look at him again.

Hold on their shared reflection. End before the moment turns into a confession or a promise.
