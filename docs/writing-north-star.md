# Story and character north star

Draconia occupies the rival role Draco fills in the source story, but she is her own character. Build her from what she wants and the choices those wants force her to make.

Her loyalties, pride, and attraction to Harry can pull in different directions. Let that conflict shape how she approaches him, avoids him, competes with him, and changes over the story. A clever scene turn should grow from this character logic and make a familiar moment mean something new. For example, Draconia could hide a vulnerable Mirror of Erised revelation with: “Your mirror is broken, Harry. I only see you and me.”

Keep the story emotionally serious even when a beat is funny. Choices should leave consequences. Let trust, forgiveness, and cooperation grow from what the characters understand and do for one another; the resolution should feel earned through their relationships and allies, not arrive as a vague spell that makes pain disappear.

This is a direction for character and scene work, not an approved beat sheet. The source essay's specific plot events remain ideas until they are checked against the feature's continuity and chosen deliberately.
