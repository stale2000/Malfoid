## Tweet text

"A love potion." Snape repeated flatly.

"I didn't say the word love," replied back Draconia, "But yes, something of that nature."

Snape sighed, but continued writing, not bothering to look up at her.

"What symptoms are you experiencing?"

"A quickening heart rate. Shakiness around the... suspect. Unusual emotional responses in the presence of the suspect. Intrusive thoughts of the suspect. Romantic ideation." Draconia's face blushed as she said the words aloud.

"And who might the 'suspect' be?"

"I don't see how that's relevan-"

"Miss Malfoid, this is a very serious accusation. It is my responsibility as a professor to inquire fully. Who is it?" Snape finally set his papers aside, looking up at her from his desk.

Draconia twiddled her wand in her fingertips, refusing to even look in his direction.

"...Potter.", she said, pushing a lock of silver hair behind her ear.

"Potter." Snape repeated.

"T-that's what I said, isn't it?"

"Potter can hardly brew a sleeping draught much less a-"

"Then Granger brewed it! Or he stole it! I don't know! Can you just test me? Or something?"

Snape stood up, seemingly annoyed by the whole ordeal. He turned, reaching into his cabinet of restricted potions, rustling through them, looking for something.

"And why, pray tell, would Potter do this to you?"

Her heart skipped a beat. She hadn't considered that. Why would he? Unless he... She shook her head, as if the thought could be shaken out of her.

"To humiliate me, obviously. I bet he's having a grand laugh right now at my expense. Stupid Potter." She clenched her fists, indignant.

Snape turned and produced a small potion of silver liquid.

"Drink this. It will cure you of whatever may, or may not be ailing you."

She swiped it from him and held it up to her lips, but paused. Why did she pause? Drinking this would cure her of the love potion, it would make these inconvenient feelings go away. So then why isn't she drinking it? She clenched her fists, and chugged the potion before she could second guess herself again. She stood there, eyes closed and cheeks pink, waiting for something to change.

"How long until it starts to wor-"

"Instantly."

She stood there for several more seconds, holding her breath, until Harry appeared in her mind again, and her pulse quickened.

Her eye twitched, "Well clearly this has been a huge waste of time."

"Obviously." Snape said flatly, the corner of his mouth twitching slightly.
