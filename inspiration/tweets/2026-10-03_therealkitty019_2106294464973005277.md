[Part 2: Aftermath]

Malfoy was inconsolable.

Harry leaned against the wall, watching as Lupin knelt down beside the sobbing girl. He laid a hand on her shoulder and spoke; "Malfoy" he said, softly, gently, the way a father would. She flinched away, but Lupin's hand remained firm. "The Boggart is gone now, there's nothing to fear. You can open your eyes."

"N-no..." she mewled, weakly. "I- I  cannot bear to see it again..." Lupin exhaled softly, then asked;

"What was it that scared you, Malfoy?" The girl rocked her lithe frame from side to side, a silent denial of his question. A moment more and Lupin sighed, quiet resignation spread across his face as he turned to Harry. "I'd like you to try." Harry was aghast.

"Me?!?" Lupin nodded, and stood beside the boy. "What do you think I can do here?" Harry said, trying his best to keep his voice, and frustration, suppressed.

"You saw the Boggart, Harry. This is something between you two and I can't interfere." Harry felt heat rise in his chest- two and a half years of petty harassment, her father trying to engineer his death the year prior, and somehow this was his problem? These thoughts contorted to a foul grimace that cut across his lips- not without Lupin's notice. "No, don't give me that look, Potter. I know yours and that girl's history- I've seen how you two joust. But, whatever you feel, it's irrelevant. Now go. Speak to her."

Harry sighed, pushing off from the cool stone wall. Heels clicked softly against the floor, slow and rhythmic as he approached. He wasn't thinking of any particular strategy, it's not like he had any experience in the matter anyways. Granger's tantrums always got foisted off to the older girls, and he and Ron had long since learned when she was in her foulest of moods- and when to avoid her.

No, this was something different.

What little he knew simply did not apply. It would be one thing if it were Ginny- at least then Ron or the twins could help- but Malfoy? Harry sighed, having finally reached the girl. What was best?- he wondered. Call out? Yell at her? God, what do I even do here? A moment of pondering passed, then another, then on the eve of the third, finally, he decided.

He thought back to the times Dumbledore and Hagrid had reassured him, and to how Lupin had knelt beside the girl minutes before. Mimicking the motion, he sat opposite to her, resting his hands on his knees as he called out gently: "Hey." She flinched again, like electricity blitzed through her veins, and shuddered. She quivered in terror, thin lines of mascara dissolved by tears now streaking down her face-

Since when did she wear makeup?

The thought came and went unbidden. He searched for words as he studied her face; a round frame, upturned nose, the slightest blush on her cheeks. A slightly too-large forehead, carefully obscured by bangs. Were it anyone else, Harry could have found a sort of tragic beauty in it. But it was Malfoy.

"We're going to be late for our next class, Malfoy. You need to get up." She shuddered again, shaking her head furiously. Harry reached for her wand- slowly, carefully, knowing well the consequences. A finger against the tip- then a second. Curling down the shaft as he moved his hand to hers. As gently as he could, pulling down her clenched hands back to her sides. He watched the wand's tip carefully, scouring it for any hint of movement or magic. Finally, with her hands lowered, her arms relaxed and Harry sighed in relief- only to find his eyes met with hers.

The air caught in his throat as Harry inhaled. Those silver eyes, once always from a distance, now close. Too close. It was like they bored through his very soul, studying every flaw, every pore, searching for something. He couldn't bear to look away as wood clattered against stone, and he couldn't bear to move as her delicate hand caressed the side of his face. The silence was oppressive, broken only by the roar of his heartbeat echoed against a gentle thump- thump- thump- that spread from her palm to his jaw.

"Are you..." The words were spoken softly and her hands moved to grasp Harry's scarf, tugging gently at it. Her eyes closed once again-inhaling- exhaling- each breath an uncertain moment that seemed to last forever and a part of Harry wished, and feared, that they would. A moment later, Dracaena Malfoy inhaled deeply and-

Blew her nose using his scarf.

"Oh come on!" Harry exclaimed, drawing a delighted giggle from Malfoy. "Feeling better now?" He sighed, as Malfoy's eyes met his.

"I was feeling fine the entire time, of course." A thin smirk to display confidence, even as the flush around her eyes revealed the lie. "I got you where I wanted though, so my win?" Harry sighed again, drawing a handkerchief from the breast of his robes.

"You'd be more convincing without all this makeup on your cheeks." He said, wiping away the smoky black lines. "I didn't think someone like you would even bother with makeup." She frowned, pouting.

"I know you don't care for it, Potter, but girls do like to be pretty now and then." His heart disagreed with the girl's words, but giving voice to the feeling was too much. "Even so- hey-" His thumb traced along her cheek, soft and warm. "- I can do it myself!" She snapped, snatching the handkerchief from Harry's hand and snapping him out of the trance. He stood, dusting himself off as Malfoy fixed her robes, Lupin appearing over the boy's shoulder.

"So, you've come to your senses, Miss Malfoy?" She turned to Lupin, haughty, seemingly drunk on confidence.

"Yes professor, and I must apologize for my shameful display. I simply do not know what came over me." That thin veil of vulnerability Harry had seen was, in an instant, torn away and he stood reminded of just what frustrated him so much about this girl.

"Good." Lupin said, glancing down to Harry. "Then, if you've collected yourselves, I'll need you to vacate the room. The next class will be arriving soon. As for the rest of your day, however, it is yours." The two cocked their heads inquisitively and Lupin snorted. "What I mean, is you've been excused from the rest of the day's classes." Malfoy seemed uncharacteristically sullen at this and Harry narrowed his eyes, waiting for the other shoe to drop. "I expect you to rest-" he started, before turning to Harry; "- and I expect you, Potter, to keep an eye on her."
