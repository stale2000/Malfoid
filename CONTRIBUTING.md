# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Project Malfoid. The project is in early development; useful contributions include feedback on the public plan, story structure, anime design notes, scene-packet formats, and production documentation.

Before opening a pull request:

- Describe the change and its effect on story, continuity, or production.
- Include source and reuse information for any outside reference, model, image, sound, or other asset.
- Do not submit the source movie, movie excerpts, extracted dialogue, third-party fan art, voice samples, or private production material.
- Keep character portrayal age-appropriate and follow the project content notes in the production plan.
- Check whether a contribution is original and whether you can license it under CC BY 4.0. Do not submit work on behalf of another creator without their permission.

Open a discussion issue before large creative or production changes so the direction can be reviewed before detailed work is done. Maintainers may ask for attribution or provenance details before accepting a contribution.
